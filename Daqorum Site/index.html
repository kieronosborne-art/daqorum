Who are Daqorum?

Daqorum are creators and publishers of accessible technology designed to remove barriers to learning and development.

Practical products

Our experience is rooted in developing practical products that solve real problems.

Built on UDL

We believe that technology should make it easier for people to access learning, express their ideas and realise their potential, from classroom to the workplace, that's why all our solutions are designed with UDL in mind.

Our solutions
